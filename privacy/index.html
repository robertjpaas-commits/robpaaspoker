Privacy policy

Updated October 22, 2026

This policy covers RobPaasPoker's own tools: RobPaasPoker Posting, which publishes my own videos to my own YouTube channel, Facebook Page, Instagram and X accounts, and my personal planner. I am their only user. They run on my own computer and sign in only to my own accounts.

YouTube API Services

RobPaasPoker Posting uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What is accessed and stored

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the signed-in channel, and to read that channel's basic details (its name), to confirm the connection.
  • Facebook, Instagram and X: permission to publish to my own Page and accounts, and to read their names.
  • My personal planner: read access to my own calendar, email and fitness data, and adding events to a calendar it created.
  • The sign-in tokens these services issue, and a local log of each post and the ID the platform returned.

All of it is stored only on my own computer. No data about viewers, other channels or other people is read or stored.

How it is used and shared

The data is used only to publish my videos and to record that they were published. It is not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party, and it is not used for advertising or analytics. The tools set no cookies and use no tracking.

Deleting data and revoking access

Access to a Google account can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page, which stops the tool from reaching YouTube. Deleting the tool's local token and log files removes everything it stored. Facebook, Instagram and X access can be removed from each service's own app settings.
