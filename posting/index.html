RobPaasPoker Posting

A private tool I built to post my own short poker videos. Each morning it takes one finished short from my computer and publishes it to my own accounts: my YouTube channel Rob Paas Poker, my Facebook Page, my Instagram and my X account.

It runs only on my own computer and signs in only to my own accounts. It isn't offered to anyone else, has no sign-up, and collects no one else's data.

YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to my channel. Using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of the data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
